THE TIME BETWEEN WORLDS

A little distance. A different past.

Light travels at 299,792.458 km/s in vacuum. Distances are calculated to the centre of Earth, accounting for the time light left the source.

Moonlight and planetary light are reflected sunlight. The delay shown starts at that reflection; the earlier journey from the Sun is not included.

The diagram compresses orbits and enlarges bodies. It does not predict sky visibility. Playback reconstructs a fixed observation and never changes your watch signal.

Body centres are used. Surface location and atmospheric effects are not modelled.

Offline estimates use Astronomy Engine. They are educational calculations, not live telescope measurements.
